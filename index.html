20x4 LCD Designer

Click and drag to draw pixels. Each LCD character is 5×8 pixels. The display is 20×4 characters (100×32 pixels total).


Convert Full Canvas to LCD-Safe Text

A real HD44780-style LCD can only show its built-in font plus up to 8 custom characters (CGRAM slots 0–7). This tool matches every 5×8 cell of your drawing against the closest built-in character, finds the shapes that don't look like any letter/number/symbol, and figures out which 8 of those shapes are worth "spending" a custom-character slot on — ranked by how much total visual error they fix across the whole screen. You can override the picks below before generating code.
